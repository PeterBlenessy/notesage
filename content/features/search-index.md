## [web]

**Find anything, instantly.** Notesage quietly keeps a live index of every note, tag, mention, task, and goal across your workspace. Search by name or by content and results come back the moment you type — no waiting, no re-scanning.

## [deep]

### What it does

Behind the scenes, Notesage keeps a live index of your whole workspace — every note's content, plus every tag, mention, task, and goal. It updates itself as files change, so search is instant and always current. Nothing is uploaded to build it; the index is rebuilt locally on each device.

### More than filenames

Because the index understands structure, it doesn't just match filenames. It tells a `#tag` from the same text in a code block, tracks every `@mention`, and automatically collects every checkbox as a task and every goal block — so your outstanding work is queryable, not scattered.

### A productivity backbone

That task and goal awareness turns a pile of notes into a lightweight productivity system: a live list of every open checkbox across all your notes, without a separate to-do app. Tick a box in any note and the list updates. Your tasks live inside the same notes as the thinking that produced them — not in a disconnected tracker.

### Example

You press {{shortcut:find-file}} to jump to a note by name; another day you open the tasks view and see every unchecked item from every meeting note in one place — then delegate a couple to your AI and mark the rest done.

### When to use it

Whenever "where did I write that?" or "what's still open?" comes up — which, in a growing workspace, is constantly.

### Tips

- Content search lives in the command bar; filename search is {{shortcut:find-file}}.
- Tags and mentions each have their own fast search.
- Only your projects and library are indexed — arbitrary folders you open aren't, by design.

## [in-app]

Press {{shortcut:find-file}} to find a file by name, or open the command bar and search your notes' content. Tasks and goals are picked up automatically, so your outstanding items are always one search away. The index rebuilds itself as files change.
