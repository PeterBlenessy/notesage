## [web]

**Find anything, change everything.** Search the document you're in — and replace as you go — whether it's a note, a PDF, an ebook, or a code file. Matches highlight instantly with a running count.

## [in-app]

Press {{shortcut:find}} to open the find bar and jump between matches; press {{shortcut:replace}} to reveal the replace row and swap the current match or all of them at once. It works across markdown, PDFs, ebooks, Office files, and code.

## [social]

⌘F to find, ⌘⇧H to replace — in your notes, and in every PDF, ebook, and code file you open in Notesage.
