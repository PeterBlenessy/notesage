## [web]

**Connect ideas as you write.** Type `#` to tag a note and `@` to mention a person or file — both become clickable badges. Later, one click gathers every place a tag or mention appears across your whole workspace, so related thinking finds its way back together.

## [in-app]

Type `#` or `@` while writing to add a tag or mention, with autocomplete. Press {{shortcut:search-tags}} to search tags and {{shortcut:search-mentions}} to search mentions across every note — each with a snippet preview that jumps you straight to the spot.

## [social]

#tag and @mention as you write, then click to see everywhere they appear across your notes. Ideas find their way back together.
