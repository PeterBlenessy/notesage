## [web]

**Nothing but the page.** Focus mode fades the sidebar, toolbar, and every bit of chrome until only your words remain. Need a hand mid-sentence? Summon the assistant, ask, and send it away again — then keep writing.

## [deep]

### What it does

Focus mode strips the interface back to the page. The sidebar, toolbar, status bar, and every other piece of chrome fade away, your text centres, and there's nothing left to look at but the words you're writing. Press the key again and everything returns exactly as it was.

### AI without leaving the flow

Focus doesn't mean cut off. Summon the command bar, ask your question, read the answer, and dismiss it — the chrome stays hidden the whole time. You get a second opinion without the visual noise of a full workspace.

### Example

You're drafting a difficult paragraph, so you press {{shortcut:focus}} and the projects list and toolbar dissolve. Halfway through you're unsure of a word — focus mode stays on as you open the command bar, ask for three alternatives, pick one, and keep typing. The interface never reasserts itself.

### When to use it

First drafts, deep edits, morning pages — anything where the interface competes with the sentence. Writers reach for it when the goal is flow, not navigation.

### Tips

- It pairs naturally with a wider editor and your preferred typography — set those once and focus mode inherits them.
- Everything still responds; you're hiding chrome, not disabling features.
- Reduced-motion is respected — the fade is instant if you've asked the system to minimise motion.

## [in-app]

Press {{shortcut:focus}} to enter or leave focus mode. The interface fades away and your text stays centred and calm. Everything still works — open the command bar, then dismiss it, and the chrome stays hidden until you ask for it back.

## [social]

Hit ⌘. and everything fades but your words. Distraction-free writing, with AI a keystroke away when you want it — and gone when you don't.
