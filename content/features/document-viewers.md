## [web]

**Read almost anything, in place.** Open PDFs, ebooks, Word and PowerPoint files, and code right inside Notesage — no second app. Turn pages, search the text, and keep your reading beside your writing.

## [in-app]

Open a PDF, ebook, Word, PowerPoint, or code file and it renders inline. Press {{shortcut:find}} to search the text, and use the toolbar to zoom or turn pages. Ebooks remember where you left off between sessions.

## [social]

PDFs, ebooks, Word, PowerPoint, code — all readable right inside Notesage. Search the text, keep your place, never leave your notes.
