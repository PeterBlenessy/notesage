## [web]

**Talk to your draft — and let AI answer.** Leave a comment on any passage, then hand it to your assistant with a click. The reply lands in the thread, you can reply back, and you can accept the suggestion straight into your document.

## [in-app]

Select some text and press {{shortcut:add-comment}} to leave a comment. Click Delegate to send it to your AI assistant; its reply appears in the thread. Reply for a back-and-forth, and use Apply to fold an accepted suggestion into the document.

## [social]
