## [web]

**Your to-dos, where you already think.** Notesage reads the checkboxes and goals out of your notes and gathers them into one live list — so your tasks live beside the work that created them, not in a separate app you forget to open. Tick a box anywhere and the list updates.

## [deep]

### What it does

Notesage treats a checkbox as a task and a goal block as a goal — automatically, wherever they appear. Instead of copying to-dos into a separate tracker, you write them where the thinking happens, and Notesage gathers every open item into one live view. Check something off in any note and it drops off the list.

### Goals, not just tasks

Bigger than a checkbox? A note marked as a goal becomes a first-class goal, with ready-made templates for OKRs, simple checklists, SMART goals, and milestone tracking. A project's goals even feed your AI's context, so the assistant knows what you're working toward.

### Hand it off to your AI

Any task can be delegated: leave a comment on it, hand it to an AI agent, and get the work — a draft, a summary, a fix — back in the thread. Your to-do list isn't just a record; it's a place to get things done.

### Example

After a week of meeting notes, you press {{shortcut:open-tasks}} and see forty checkboxes scattered across a dozen notes in one place. You tick off the done ones, delegate three follow-ups to your assistant, and see at a glance what's still open — without a single item living outside your notes.

### When to use it

Any time your work generates action items — meetings, projects, reading, planning. It shines when tasks would otherwise scatter across a dozen documents and quietly get forgotten.

### Tips

- Press {{shortcut:open-tasks}} to open the tasks view any time.
- Mark a note as a goal with `type: goal` frontmatter to track objectives, not just to-dos.
- Delegate a task from a comment to turn a checkbox into finished work.

## [in-app]

Press {{shortcut:open-tasks}} to open the tasks view — a live list of every unchecked box across your notes. Notes with `type: goal` frontmatter appear as goals with their own templates (OKRs, checklists, SMART, milestones). Tick items off in place, or delegate a task to your AI from a comment.
