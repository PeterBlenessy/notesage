## [web]

**Your files, your structure.** Open any folder of notes and start working — or group related work into projects, each with its own settings and AI context. Want your notes on every device? Turn on iCloud sync per project; leave it off and nothing ever leaves your Mac.

## [in-app]

Press {{shortcut:open-folder}} to open a folder, or {{shortcut:new-project}} to create a project with its own metadata and AI context. Enable iCloud sync per project in its settings — it's off by default, so syncing is always your choice.

## [social]

Open any folder, organise into projects, sync via iCloud if you want to — per project, opt-in. Your files stay yours.
