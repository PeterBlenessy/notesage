## [web]

**From sources to synthesis.** Clip a web page to clean markdown, save it with tags, and it joins a searchable research library. When you're ready, ask the assistant to read across your sources and draft a synthesis — then drop in a formatted citation with a click.

## [in-app]

Press {{shortcut:research}} (or type `?` in the command bar) to search your research library by keyword or tag. Use the research skills to download a page, save it, synthesise across sources, and insert a citation. Research lives in a `research/` folder you own.

## [social]

Clip sources, tag them, search them — then let AI synthesise across your whole research library, citations included.
