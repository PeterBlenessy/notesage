## [web]

**Your notes, as a web of ideas.** Link one note to another and Notesage tracks it both ways — every note shows what links to it and what it links out to. Hover a link to preview the target without leaving the page, and a folder of notes starts to read like a wiki.

## [in-app]

Link to another note with a normal markdown link or the `[[` wiki-link helper. The Relations panel shows *Linked from* and *Links to* for the open document, and hovering an internal link previews the target. Links resolve across your whole workspace.

## [social]

Backlinks and relations turn a folder of notes into a navigable wiki — hover any link to peek, and see everything that points back.
