## [web]

**Send something that looks finished.** Turn any note into a polished PDF, a Word document, a slide deck, or a shareable web page — in one step. Tables line up, headings look considered, and no one would guess it began as plain text.

## [deep]

### What it does

Export turns the note you're looking at into a finished, shareable document — in one step, with no template-wrangling. The styling comes from your editor, so what you see on screen is what lands in the file. Four formats cover almost every need.

### Four formats

- **PDF** — professionally typeset, with Clean, Academic, and Report templates.
- **Word (.docx)** — fully editable in Word or Pages.
- **PowerPoint (.pptx)** — a deck built from your headings and bullets.
- **HTML** — a self-contained page you can share or publish.

### It carries the rich stuff

Tables keep their formatting and totals, callouts stay styled, charts and drawings render, and code keeps its highlighting. The reader gets a polished document; only you know it started as plain markdown.

### Example

A freelancer drafts a client brief in calm markdown, presses {{shortcut:export}}, picks *PDF → Report*, and sends a title-paged, typeset document — tables aligned, headings numbered — minutes later.

### When to use it

Any time the audience shouldn't see raw markdown: client deliverables, hand-ins, proposals, slide decks, or a web page from a note.

### Tips

- Set your typography once in the editor; every export inherits it.
- Choose page size (A4 / Letter / A5) and toggle a table of contents and page numbers per export.
- HTML export is self-contained — no external fonts or scripts — so it opens anywhere.

## [in-app]

Press {{shortcut:export}} (or choose Export from the toolbar) to open the export dialog. Pick a format — PDF, Word, PowerPoint, or HTML — choose a template and page size, and save. The styling comes from your editor, so what you see is what you get.
