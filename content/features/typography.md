## [web]

**Make it yours to read.** Pick from beautiful reading fonts — or any font on your Mac — and tune size, line height, and spacing until the page feels right. What you set is what you get: your typography carries through to every PDF and document you export.

## [in-app]

Open the typography controls from the editor toolbar (or **Settings → Editor**) to set the font, size, line height, and paragraph spacing. Choose from curated presets or any installed system font. Your choices are saved, and they flow through to exports too.

## [social]

Your words, your typeface. Pick a reading font, dial in the spacing, and it carries all the way through to export.
