## [web]

**Give your assistant new abilities.** Skills add reusable capabilities — research a topic, format a citation, run a small script — and agents are specialised assistants you can address by name. Both follow open standards shared across today's AI tools, so what you build isn't locked to Notesage.

## [in-app]

Type `/` in the command bar to run a skill, or `@` to address an agent. Skills and agents are discovered from your projects and the tools you've connected; manage them in **Settings → Skills & Agents**. Add your own, or use the ones your AI provider ships.

## [social]
