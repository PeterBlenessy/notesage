# About Notesage

**Notesage** is a private, local-first writing tool with built-in AI collaboration. Write in rich markdown, connect any AI you already use, and keep your notes on your own device.

## What's new in this release

This version brings improved PDF and document export, smoother AI chat streaming, and performance improvements across the editor and sidebar.
