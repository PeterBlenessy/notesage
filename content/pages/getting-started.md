# Getting Started

From download to your first AI-assisted note in five minutes.

---

## Step 1: Install Notesage

1. Download the latest Notesage `.dmg` from the [releases page](https://github.com/PeterBlenessy/notesage/releases).
2. Open the `.dmg`, drag Notesage to your **Applications** folder, and launch it.
3. On first launch, macOS may ask you to confirm you want to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click **Open**.

---

## Step 2: Open a folder

Notesage works with folders of markdown files — your existing notes, a new folder, anything you like.

1. Press **⌘O** (or click **Open Folder** in the welcome screen).
2. Choose any folder on your computer. Notesage will display its files in the sidebar.

> **Tip:** If you don't have a folder yet, create a new one anywhere — Desktop, Documents, wherever suits you. Notesage will create notes inside it.

---

## Step 3: Connect an AI provider

You can write notes without any AI connection, but to use chat and assistance you'll need to add one.

1. Open **Settings** (**⌘,**) and click **Connections**.
2. Click **Add Connection** and choose your provider:
   - **Anthropic or OpenAI:** Paste an API key from their website.
   - **GitHub Copilot, Gemini, or Codex:** Sign in with your existing subscription.
   - **Ollama:** Install Ollama on your computer, then add the connection (no key needed).
   - **Bundled model:** Enable Local AI — no account required.
3. Click **Save**. The connection appears in your list, ready to use.

---

## Step 4: Take your first note

1. Click the **+** button in the sidebar (or press **⌘N**) to create a new note.
2. Start typing. Use the toolbar to format text, or type `/` to insert a callout, table, or drawing.
3. Press **⌘K** to open the AI command bar. Type a question — the AI will reply instantly.
4. Your note saves automatically as you type.

That's it. Your note is a plain `.md` file in your folder, ready to open in any text editor.

---

## What's next?

- **Explore keyboard shortcuts:** Press **⌘⇧K** to see the most useful ones.
- **Create a project:** Press **⌘⇧N** to create a dedicated folder with its own AI context.
- **Try focus mode:** Press **⌘.** to hide all the interface and just write.
- **Export your note:** Press **⌘⇧E** to export to PDF, Word, or PowerPoint.
