# Made for the way you work

Notesage adapts to how you think and write — not the other way around. Here is what that looks like in practice.

---

## Write without distraction

Focus mode fades everything but your words. No tabs, no toolbars, no clutter — just the page. When a thought needs a second opinion, summon the assistant with a single keystroke, ask, and send it away again. The interface stays quiet so your writing can be the loudest thing in the room.

---

## Keep every project — and every client — separate

Each project is its own space, with its own notes, settings, and AI. Tie a project to a single provider and its material never mixes with another's — so confidential client work stays sealed off from everything else. When it's finished, a project is just a folder you can hand over, archive, or delete.

---

## Turn conversations into notes

Record a meeting or a quick spoken summary, and Notesage transcribes the whole thing for you — automatically, on your own device. No uploading audio to a service, no copying and pasting from another app. What was said becomes a note you can edit, tag, and act on.

---

## Read, annotate, and make sense of it

Open PDFs, ebooks, and research papers right inside Notesage. Highlight a passage, leave a comment, and ask the assistant to explain or summarise it — the reply appears alongside your note, ready to fold into your own writing. Tag related ideas as you go, and a single click gathers every note on a topic across your whole library.

---

## Send something that looks finished

Any note becomes a polished document in one step — a typeset PDF, a Word file, or a slide deck. Tables line up, headings look considered, and no one would guess it began as plain text. Draft in calm simplicity; deliver something professional.

---

## Your work stays yours

Every note is an ordinary file on your computer — no account, no lock-in, nothing uploaded behind your back. Connect the AI you already pay for, bring your own key, or run a model entirely offline. However you work, your words stay on your device unless you choose to send them.
