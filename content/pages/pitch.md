# Notesage

**The writing tool that thinks with you.**

Notesage is a fast, private desktop app that combines a beautiful rich-text editor with powerful AI collaboration. Write your ideas, connect any AI model you already use, and let the two work together — all without your notes ever leaving your computer.

## Why Notesage?

- **Your notes stay yours.** Everything is stored locally on your device. No cloud account required, no data uploaded behind your back.
- **Work with any AI, your way.** Bring your own API key, use your existing Claude, Copilot, or Gemini subscription, or run a fully offline model — Notesage connects to all of them.
- **Beautiful writing, powerful thinking.** A rich markdown editor, AI chat, and a searchable knowledge base, all in one lightweight app.
