# Attention and Devotion

> Attention is the rarest and purest form of generosity.

**Key idea.** The essay argues that sustained attention is itself a kind of care — that to attend fully to something is to grant it a small devotion. The writer traces the thought from old contemplative sources through modern studies of focus.

**Why it matters for the piece.** This is the hinge for [On Attention](<../Essays/On Attention.md>): attention as devotion, not merely productivity.
