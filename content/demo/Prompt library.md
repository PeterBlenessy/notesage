# Prompt library

Reusable prompts I reach for while writing.

## Tighten

> Tighten this passage without losing its voice. Prefer shorter sentences, and keep the last line.

## Second opinion

> Read this as a skeptical but kind editor. What is the weakest paragraph, and why?

## Outline from notes

> Turn these rough notes into a three-part outline. Keep my phrasing where it's good.
