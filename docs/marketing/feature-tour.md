# Feature Tour

A quick look at what makes Notesage the writing tool that thinks with you.

---

## Editor — Write Beautifully

The Notesage editor feels like a premium writing surface. Type in plain markdown or switch to rich text at any time — your words always stay exactly as you wrote them.

- **Formatting made easy:** Bold, italic, headings, lists, code blocks, tables, and more — all with keyboard shortcuts or a floating toolbar.
- **Slash commands:** Type `/` anywhere to insert a callout block, table, drawing, chart, or image in seconds.
- **Callouts:** Highlight important content with Note, Tip, Warning, or Important callout blocks.
- **Charts and drawings:** Embed live charts and Excalidraw drawings directly in your notes. No separate app needed.
- **Link previews:** Paste a URL and Notesage fetches the title, description, and image — turning any link into a rich preview card.
- **Dynamic tables:** Tables that sort, filter, and calculate totals right inside your note.

## AI Chat & Quiet Composer

Talk to your AI assistant without leaving your document. The Quiet Composer's floating command bar sits at the bottom of the screen — press **⌘K** or double-tap **⌘** and start typing.

- **Prefix shortcuts:** Type `#` to search tags, `@` to reference people or files, `?` to search your research, `/` to run a skill.
- **Agent Orb:** A small ambient indicator in the corner shows when a background AI task is running. Click it to see what's happening.
- **Pin the chat panel:** Prefer a side panel? Pin the command bar to the right edge of the window for a permanent chat view.

![AI chat & Quiet Composer](screenshots/ai-chat.png)

## Sidebar & Projects

Navigate your workspace with ease using the flat, curated sidebar.

- **Pinned files:** One click to your most-visited notes.
- **Projects:** Organised folders with AI context, skills, and settings per project.
- **Recent:** The last files you touched, always within reach.
- **Tags & Mentions:** Jump to any `#tag` or `@mention` across all your notes in one click.
- **Folder Peek:** Hover over a project to see its top-level contents without opening it.

![Sidebar & projects](screenshots/sidebar.png)

## Document Index & Search

Notesage keeps a live index of every tag, mention, task, and goal across all your notes — so you can find anything instantly.

- **Tag search (`⌘3`):** See every note that uses a given tag, with a snippet preview.
- **Mention search (`⌘2`):** Track every `@person` reference across your workspace.
- **Full-text search:** The command bar's file search finds notes by name or content.
- **Tasks and goals:** Notesage automatically discovers checkboxes and goal blocks, so you always know what's outstanding.

## Export

Turn any note into a polished document in seconds.

- **PDF:** Professionally typeset with three templates — Clean, Academic, and Report.
- **Word (.docx):** Open and edit in Microsoft Word or Pages.
- **PowerPoint (.pptx):** Generate a presentation from headings and bullet points.
- **HTML:** Self-contained, shareable web page with full formatting and syntax highlighting.

Press **⌘⇧E** or choose **Export** from the toolbar to open the export dialog.

![Export dialog](screenshots/export-dialog.png)

## Voice Transcription & Dictation

Speak your notes instead of typing them.

- **Live dictation:** Press **⌘⇧R** to start — your words appear in the document as you speak.
- **Meeting recording:** Record a conversation, then transcribe it with a single click.
- **Offline transcription:** Powered by on-device Whisper — no audio is sent to any server.
- **99 languages supported.**

![Voice transcription](screenshots/voice-transcription.png)

## Document Viewers

Open almost any document type right inside Notesage.

- **EPUB:** Read ebooks with paginated or scroll mode, bookmarks, and search.
- **PDF:** View PDFs with text search and zoom.
- **Word & PowerPoint:** High-fidelity viewers for `.docx` and `.pptx` files.
- **Code files:** Syntax-highlighted editor for 22+ programming languages.

![Document viewer](screenshots/document-viewer.png)

## Comments & AI Delegation

Add inline comments to any text, then hand them off to your AI agent for a response.

- Highlight text, press **⌘⇧M**, and type your comment.
- Click **Delegate** to send the comment to your AI assistant.
- The AI replies in-thread — you can reply back for a full conversation.
- Accept the suggestion directly into your document with one click.
