# Use Cases

Real ways people use Notesage every day.

---

## The Researcher: Synthesising 20 Papers

**Maya** is a PhD student in environmental science. She downloads papers as PDFs and opens them directly in Notesage's built-in viewer. As she reads, she adds inline comments on key passages — then delegates them to her AI assistant with one click.

"Summarise this finding in plain language" → the AI replies in the comment thread, and Maya can accept the summary directly into her notes.

At the end of the week she has a research note full of her own thoughts and AI-generated summaries, all tagged `#climate-policy`. One click on that tag shows every related note across her project.

---

## The Writer: Distraction-Free Quiet Composer

**Lena** writes long-form essays. She discovered Notesage's focus mode (**⌘.**), which fades out all the surrounding interface so only her words remain.

When she gets stuck, she doesn't open another app. She presses **⌘K**, types her question in the floating command bar, and the AI responds inline. Then she closes the bar and keeps writing.

Her writing stays on her Mac. She never pastes her drafts into a browser.

---

## The Developer: Project Knowledge Base

**Kai** maintains a software project. He keeps a `docs/` folder of architecture notes, decision logs, and meeting summaries — all in Notesage.

Whenever code changes, he asks the AI to update the relevant document. He uses the `#decision` tag to mark architectural choices, and the tag search shows the full history instantly.

When a new team member joins, Kai shares the folder. They open it in their own copy of Notesage.

---

## The Student: Building a Study System

**Jordan** is studying for professional exams. After each lecture, she opens Notesage and dictates a quick summary (**⌘⇧R**) while the material is fresh. The on-device transcription turns her spoken notes into text in seconds.

She then asks the AI to turn her rough notes into flashcard-style questions. The questions go into a `#flashcards` section she reviews before each exam.

No notes leave her laptop — important, since her coursework is confidential.

---

## The Freelancer: Client Deliverables

**Alex** is a freelance copywriter. Each client gets its own Notesage project with a locked AI provider — so client A's content never touches the same AI context as client B's.

When a client requests a content brief, Alex drafts it in Notesage, then uses the one-click export to send a polished PDF. Tables auto-format, headings look professional, and the client never knows the underlying file is plain markdown.

---

## The Team Lead: Meeting Notes and Action Items

**Sam** runs a busy product team. During meetings, Notesage's voice recording captures everything. Afterward, Sam transcribes it, and the document index automatically extracts every checkbox as a task item.

**⌘1** opens the tasks view — a live list of every open checkbox across all notes. Sam assigns actions in comments, delegates follow-ups to the AI, and marks them done with one click.
