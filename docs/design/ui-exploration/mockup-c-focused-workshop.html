On Attention

Peter Blenessy · saved 40 s ago · 2,184 words

The hardest part of thinking is not the thinking itself but holding still long enough for a thought to arrive. Distraction is rarely loud. It is almost always polite, small, well-intended — a little interruption wearing a useful face.

I have been reading old essays on the discipline of writing, and one line has kept returning to me: attention is the beginning of devotion.

Note There are three density presets above the tab strip. Compact is Linear-dense. Comfortable is the default. Spacious gives generous air for long-form drafting. Every component scales together — no one-off exceptions.

What the tools are for

A good writing surface should disappear. Density is not a "setting" in the usual sense — it's a mode you inhabit depending on what you're doing. Reviewing? Compact. Drafting? Spacious.

  • Sidebar is a flat list of pinned + recent + tags — no trees to expand.
  • Tabs are Linear-muted: no icons, active gets a hairline underline.
  • AI lives in the activity rail on the right — collapsible, never in your way.
