On Attention

Draft · saved 40 s ago

The hardest part of thinking is not the thinking itself but holding still long enough for a thought to arrive. Distraction is rarely loud. It is almost always polite, small, well-intended.

What the tools are for

A good writing surface should disappear. It should feel closer to a sheet of paper than a screen. When the interface fades, what remains is the voice.

  • Less chrome. More margin.
  • Type ahead — commands come to you.
  • Answers unfold above the page, not beside it.
