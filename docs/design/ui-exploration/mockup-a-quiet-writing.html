On Attention

Draft · 4 min read · saved 2 minutes ago

The hardest part of thinking is not the thinking itself but holding still long enough for a thought to arrive. Distraction is rarely loud. It is almost always polite, small, well-intended — a little interruption wearing a useful face.

I have been reading old essays on the discipline of writing, and one line has kept returning to me: attention is the beginning of devotion. I suspect the reverse is also true.

What the tools are for

A good writing surface should disappear. It should feel, as the designers of iA Writer once put it, "closer to a sheet of paper than a screen." When the interface fades, what remains is the voice.

"We write to know what we think. The page is the mirror."

This is why I keep coming back to markdown — not because it is the fastest or the most powerful format, but because it refuses to get in the way. Ceremony is the enemy of clarity.|
